# M3 storefront direction — 29 October 2026

GOOBER serves people comparing digital tools and services. In this build every product, balance and delivery is synthetic. The page must explain that context and make browsing, terms and account entry easy without suggesting real entitlements.

Preserve forest #155d43, navy #162b36, warm canvas #f8f7f3, white surfaces and existing readable muted/error colors. Georgia supplies display character; system sans supplies controls and descriptions. No external font, Apple asset, stock photograph or animation dependency is needed. Keep motion minimal and user-triggered.

Replace the repeated hero/journey composition with a literal offer, concise audience/task description and real browse/create/sign-in routes. Make the catalog the principal content: actual category, name, description, exact price and terms. Remove tall decorative Create/Explore artwork. Bound initially visible records while keeping all fetched records searchable and progressively available. Consolidate process guidance below the catalog. Align product detail with useful terms and the existing authoritative quote flow.

On 360/390px use one column and readable controls; 768px supports two product columns; 1280px supports three within a bounded shell. Preserve semantic headings, skip link, keyboard access, visible focus, labels/autocomplete, reduced motion and large-price wrapping. A failed read gets a calm notice and explicit retry; retry pending is disabled. Successful empty and filter-empty states remain distinct from errors. No background failure may steal focus. No retry automatically repeats an economic POST.
